# Phase 53 - Toolbar and Label Cleanup

The 3D viewer toolbar was moved into a compact top toolbar outside the model center.

- Camera presets are selected from a compact View dropdown.
- FOV, Target, Edges, Labels and Fullscreen are explicit toggles.
- Geometry Details is a collapsed drawer by default.
- Tactical labels are hidden in Clean label mode.
- CAD material details remain in CAD Debug mode.

Default view no longer places large geometry truth panels or label boxes over the model.
