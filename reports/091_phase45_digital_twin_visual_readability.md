# Phase 45 Digital Twin Visual Readability

The Phase 45 3D panel remains an honest read-only operational visualization.

## Improvements

- The FOV fill was reduced further and is now mostly wireframe, so it no longer dominates the turret.
- The no-go/person safety volume is visible as a faint red reference in KTR demo mode and stronger only when blocked.
- Target and ray labels remain compact and do not claim calibrated range.
- The scene keeps perspective grid, horizon/depth cues, camera optical axis, launcher reference axis, and the 30 mm camera-to-launcher offset.

## Truth Boundary

Visible model wording remains `STL-derived simplified digital twin` when simplified geometry is used for readability. This is not presented as a full-fidelity physics simulation.
