# Phase 52 - Full 3D World Route

`/cockpit/world` is now the primary 3D inspection route. It hides the camera feed and bottom cards, keeps a compact header and safety strip, and gives the 3D canvas nearly the full available page height.

Supported routes:

- `/cockpit/world?quality=ultra&mode=freecad`
- `/cockpit/world?quality=ultra&mode=showcase`
- `/cockpit/world?quality=ultra&mode=tactical`

Orbit, pan, zoom, double-click reset, preset views, and keyboard shortcuts remain active.
