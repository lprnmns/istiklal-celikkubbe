# Phase 53 - Camera Panel Reposition

The camera HUD remains available but no longer competes with the 3D digital twin for the top viewport.

- Camera HUD is below the 3D hero on `/cockpit`.
- Camera can be collapsed.
- Camera section can request browser fullscreen.
- YOLO ON/OFF remains a perception-only toggle.
- YOLO OFF is labelled as camera-only and does not claim target detection.

No camera or YOLO UI path enables physical command generation.
