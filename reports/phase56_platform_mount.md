# Phase 56 Platform Mount

Implemented a raised air-defense installation platform under the KTR digital twin:

- Dark metal rectangular deck, slightly below the calibrated device support plane.
- Four support legs down to the terrain.
- Beveled/lipped platform edges.
- Bolted mounting ring under the turret area.
- Small equipment box and cable channel for scale.
- Subtle warning stripe on the deck edge.

Placement:
- The KTR model remains at the manual calibrated transform.
- Terrain is lowered below the mounting platform.
- The platform top is aligned to the calibrated support height so the device no longer reads as a toy sitting directly on an abstract grid.

Safety:
- Platform geometry is render-only.
- No physical command path or hardware control was added.
