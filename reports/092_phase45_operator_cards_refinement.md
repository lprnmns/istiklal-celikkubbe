# Phase 45 Operator Cards Refinement

Bottom cards were adjusted to read as mission summaries rather than debug cards.

## Card Intent

- Device Manager: Camera, USB camera, Pico, Digital Twin asset, Command Path.
- Runtime / Model: Detector, Classes, Tracker, Confidence/NMS, Truth Source.
- Target / Engagement: Selected target, Class, Confidence, Direction, Fire Gate.
- Scene Plan: FOV, Projection, Offset, No-Go Zone, Sync.
- Replay & Evidence: Mode, Screenshot Folder, Asset Manifest, Projection Contract, Safety Boundary.
- Operator Log: Latest 3-4 operational events only; full logs remain available elsewhere.

Repeated safety strings are avoided in mission cards. The global safety strip remains the authoritative command-boundary statement.
